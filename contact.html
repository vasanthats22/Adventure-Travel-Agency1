FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions. Can't find what you're looking for? Just drop us a message above.

How far in advance should I book my adventure?
For popular treks like Everest Base Camp or Kilimanjaro, we recommend booking 3–6 months in advance, especially during peak season (March–May and September–November). For custom safaris and private trips, 2–3 months is usually enough.
Do I need prior experience to join a trek or expedition?
Most of our trips are designed for a range of fitness levels. Beginner-friendly treks and safaris need no prior experience — just basic fitness. High-altitude climbs and technical routes require experience and a medical check.
What's included in the trip cost?
Every trip includes expert guides, permits, accommodation, meals on the trail, safety equipment, ground transport, and 24/7 support. International flights, travel insurance, personal gear, tips, and personal expenses are not included.
Can I customize my itinerary?
Absolutely — custom trips are our specialty. Tell us your dates, interests, budget, and pace, and we'll design a completely tailored itinerary with private guides, custom routes, and special experiences.
What is your cancellation and refund policy?
Cancel 60+ days before departure for a full refund (minus a small admin fee). 30–60 days: 50% refund. Under 30 days: no refund, but you can transfer your booking to another traveller or defer to a future date.
